\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{RQ1 (separation).} A single global model yields one group (mean frequency-weighted precision 0.90) versus a hidden $k$ of up to 5. Classical-variant grouping produces 109$\times$ more groups than $\kappa$ and still mixes 1.29 distinct enabled-activity signatures per (group, variant) (versus 1.00 for $\kappa$); exact translucent-variant grouping produces 122$\times$ more.
\item \textbf{RQ1 (recovery).} $\kappa$ recovers the hidden context with mean ARI 1.00 (NMI 1.00, purity 1.00) and exactly $k$ views, using no cluster count, distance function, or threshold. Trace clustering \emph{given} $k=\mathrm{GT}$ reaches at most ARI 0.42 on average (0.57 for the best of the four); classical- and translucent-variant grouping reach ARI 0.23 / 0.32.
\item \textbf{RQ2 (model quality).} Per-family means: $\kappa$'s per-view models attain fitness 1.00 / precision 0.93 / F1 0.96 with 3.7 models (translucent F1 0.99), versus classic F1 0.94 for the single global model and 0.97 at 487 models for variant discovery; the global and variant-discovery models reach translucent F1 only 0.94 / 0.92. Case-weighted (dominated by the two \emph{enterprise} logs, $\approx$$37\%$ of cases), $\kappa$'s classic / translucent F1 are 0.97 / 0.99 versus 0.96 / 0.94 for \emph{global}.
\item \textbf{RQ3 (enabled-set noise).} $\kappa$ is exact at zero noise (ARI 1.00, 4 views) but degrades sharply as the enabled sets are perturbed: by noise level 0.4 its ARI is 0.05 and its view count has risen to 648. Control-flow trace clustering is insensitive to this noise (mean ARI 0.45 across all levels) but never recovers the context. Enabled-set fidelity is thus a precondition for $\kappa$ (see Threats to Validity).
\item \textbf{RQ3 (recorded context).} If the view-defining context is recorded as a \emph{clean} case attribute \emph{and} $k$ is known, attribute clustering also reaches ARI 0.82; on the \emph{noisy} attributes it is at chance (ARI 0.20), and on clean$+$noisy it drops to 0.64. Clustering on enabled activities alone reaches ARI 0.57. $\kappa$ needs neither a recorded attribute nor $k$ and still reaches ARI 1.00.
\item \textbf{Case study.} On the synthetic task-mining helpdesk log $\kappa$ separates the 2 hidden roles with ARI 1.00; the supervisor view exposes the privileged \texttt{escalate} / \texttt{approve\_refund} actions absent from the agent view.
\end{itemize}
